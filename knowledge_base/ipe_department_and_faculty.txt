# Department of Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE)
# Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)

**Document Version:** April 2026 (Updated based on 18th Academic Council Meeting)

## Department Overview
The Department of Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE) at BAUST provides an advanced, industry-aligned curriculum combining core mechanical/manufacturing engineering principles with modern industrial management, automation, operations research, and quality engineering.

---

## General Information
### Introduction
BAUST was established on 15 February 2015 by the Bangladesh Armed Forces to provide high-quality technical education. The university is located at Saidpur Cantonment, Saidpur, Nilphamari. The university offers four-year Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degrees in various engineering disciplines.

### Academic Objectives
*   To provide undergraduate education in CSE, EEE, ME, IPE, and CE.
*   To produce skilled, disciplined, and self-motivated engineers.
*   To foster research and development in science and technology.

### Admission Qualifications (B.Sc. Engg.)
1.  **HSC/Alim:** Minimum GPA 3.0 (with additional subject).
2.  **SSC/Dakhil:** Minimum GPA 3.0.
3.  **Combined GPA:** Minimum 7.0 (SSC + HSC).
4.  **GCE O/A Level:** Minimum C Grade in 5 O-Level subjects; Minimum C Grade in 2 A-Level subjects (Math, Physics/Chemistry). Total GPA 6.0.
5.  **Diploma Holders:** Minimum CGPA 3.0 in Diploma and GPA 3.0 in SSC.
6.  **Direct Admission:** Applicants with a total GPA of 9.0 or more in SSC/HSC are eligible for direct admission.

### Admission Test Syllabus (100 Marks, 2 Hours)
*   **Format:** Written and MCQ
*   **Mathematics:** 40%
*   **Physics:** 30%
*   **Chemistry:** 20%
*   **English:** 10%
