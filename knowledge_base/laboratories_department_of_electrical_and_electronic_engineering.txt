## Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering (EEE)
### Laboratory Facilities

The Department of Electrical and Electronic Engineering at BAUST maintains state-of-the-art laboratory facilities to support academic research and practical skill development.

| Laboratory Name | Focus Area |
| :--- | :--- |
| **DC Circuit Laboratory** | Fundamental Circuit Analysis |
| **AC Circuit Laboratory** | Alternating Current Systems |
| **Electronics Laboratory** | Semiconductor Devices & Circuits |
| **Measurement and Instrumentations Laboratory** | Precision Measurement & Sensors |
| **Electrical Machine Laboratory** | Motors, Generators & Transformers |
| **Power System Laboratory** | Power Generation, Transmission & Distribution |
| **Switchgear and Protection Laboratory** | System Safety & Fault Analysis |
| **Communication Laboratory** | Signal Processing & Telecommunications |
| **Computer and Simulation Laboratory** | Software-based Circuit Simulation |
| **Digital Logic Design Lab** | Digital Systems & Microprocessors |
| **Power Electronics Lab** | Power Conversion & Control |
