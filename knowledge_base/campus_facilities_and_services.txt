# Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)
## Campus Facilities, Infrastructure & Services

---

## 1. Digital Infrastructure & Connectivity
BAUST provides high-speed internet access to support the academic and research needs of students, faculty, and staff.
*   **Availability:** 24/7 access.
*   **Coverage:** Entire campus premises and all residential halls.
*   **Multimedia Facilities:** All classrooms are equipped with modern multimedia projectors to facilitate the visualization of theoretical concepts, animations, and e-books. Specialized multimedia support is also integrated into computer programming and simulation laboratories.

---

## 2. University Cafeteria Services
The BAUST cafeteria provides a well-furnished, hygienic dining environment for the university community.

### Overview
*   **Target Audience:** Students, Faculty Members, and Administrative Staff.
*   **Operational Hours:** During official university working hours.

### Available Services
| Service Category | Offerings |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Breakfast** | Available during morning hours |
| **Lunch** | Full meal options during midday break |

| **Fast Food** | Selection of snacks and quick-service items |

## 3. BAUST Daycare Centre
A dedicated facility designed to support the children of faculty members and staff.

### Location & Logistics
*   **Building:** Bir Protik Taramon Bibi Hall
*   **Floor:** Ground Floor

### Services & Curriculum
*   **Developmental Focus:** Guided instruction, early childhood development, and creative play.
*   **Curriculum Highlights:**
    *   Identification of primary colors.
    *   Introduction to basic food groups.
    *   Learning parts of the human body.
*   **Facilities:** Dedicated spaces for recreation, rest, and creative craft-making sessions.

*Note: For enrollment or operational inquiries, please contact the BAUST Administrative Office or the Bir Protik Taramon Bibi Hall administration.*

---

## 4. Fire Safety Policy & Infrastructure
BAUST maintains a comprehensive fire safety framework to ensure the security of the university community and assets.

### University Commitments
*   **Risk Management:** Regular fire risk assessments and mitigation strategies.
*   **Training:** Staff training on fire safety and the use of extinguishing equipment.
*   **Emergency Preparedness:** Implementation of evacuation plans, regular testing of procedures, and maintenance of safety equipment.
*   **Compliance:** Regular inspections and active liaison with local fire authorities.

### Campus Fire Safety Infrastructure
| Infrastructure Component | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fire Extinguishers** | Immediate fire suppression |
| **Fire Hydrants** | High-volume water supply for firefighting |
| **Fire Lanes** | Unobstructed access for emergency vehicles |
| **Fire Alarm Pull Stations** | Immediate notification of fire emergencies |
| **Emergency Fire Exit Doors** | Safe and rapid evacuation routes |
| **Evacuation Plans** | Strategic guidance for building clearance |

*Note: For further information or to report a safety concern, please contact the University Administration or the Proctorial Office.*
