## 2. Department of Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE)

### Overview
Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE) is the branch of engineering dedicated to the maximum utilization of limited resources. In a competitive global market, IPE focuses on increasing productivity, reducing costs, and enhancing quality and management. BAUST launched the IPE undergraduate program in 2016 to meet the growing demand for industrial engineers in sectors such as garments, textiles, pharmaceuticals, steel, and cement.

### Accreditation & Recognition
The Department of Industrial & Production Engineering (IPE) at BAUST is officially accredited by the **Institution of Engineers, Bangladesh (IEB)** under the **Board of Accreditation for Engineering and Technical Education (BAETE)**. This program is recognized under the **Washington Accord**, affirming its compliance with internationally recognized standards of engineering education.

| Criteria | Status |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Accrediting Body** | IEB-BAETE |
| **International Recognition** | Washington Accord |
| **Program Focus** | Outcome-Based Education (OBE) |

### Vision and Mission
*   **Vision:** To provide world-class education, serve as a model of excellence in research and administrative services, and prepare students for successful careers in industry and academia.
*   **Mission:**
    *   **Quality Education:** To offer high-quality, modern industrial engineering education and engage in creative research.
    *   **Professional Development:** To nurture professionals with solid technical capability, critical thinking, ethical values, and social responsibility.
    *   **Societal Impact:** To develop graduates who demonstrate continuous improvement in professional knowledge and problem-solving abilities.
    *   **Leadership:** To instill leadership qualities by integrating people, information, materials, and equipment to produce innovative and efficient organizations.
    *   **Global Standards:** To foster an environment of innovation and technical proficiency, ensuring students are equipped to excel in the modern industrial landscape while maintaining the highest ethical standards.

### Program Educational Objectives (PEOs)
*   **PEO-1:** Successfully utilize industrial engineering knowledge and techniques to solve complex real-world problems.
*   **PEO-2:** Contribute to the design and improvement of efficiency, productivity, and quality in industrial or public sectors.
*   **PEO-3:** Serve as managers or leaders who act as change agents for sustainable development.
*   **PEO-4:** Pursue advanced professional degrees, higher studies, research, or engineering certifications.

    *   **Direct Admission:** Total GPA (SSC+HSC) ≥ 9.0.
