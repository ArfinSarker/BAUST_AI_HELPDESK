## Admission & Academic Guidelines

### Admission Requirements
#### For Bangladeshi Students
*   **HSC/Alim or equivalent:** Minimum GPA 3.0 (with additional subject).
*   **Combined GPA (SSC+HSC):** Minimum 6.0 (with additional subjects).
*   **GCE O/A Level:** Minimum GPA 3.0 in both levels; Combined GPA of 6.0. No 'F' grade allowed in any subject.
*   **Admission Test:** Admission is subject to qualifying the University Admission Test.

#### For Foreign Students
*   Minimum 50% marks in 12 years of schooling.
*   English proficiency: IELTS 5.0 or TOEFL 79 (iBT).
*   Must apply within two years of completing the 12-year curriculum.
*   **Mandatory:** Medical clearance (no contagious/cardiac diseases).

### Admission Procedure & Policies
*   **Application:** Forms available at the Admission Office (Room 104, Admin Building) or via the official website.
*   **Selection:** Based on total GPA of SSC/HSC or O/A Levels.
*   **Medical & Dope Test:** Mandatory medical checkup at BAUST/CMH, Saidpur Cantonment. A "Dope Test" is required for all new students to ensure a drug-free campus.
*   **Credit Transfer:** Eligible from recognized universities; requires transcript evaluation and approval from the Equivalence Committee. Minimum 'C+' grade required for course waiver.

### Grading System
| Numerical Markings | Grade | Grade Points |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 80% and above | A+ | 4.00 |
| 75% to below 80% | A | 3.75 |
| 70% to below 75% | A- | 3.50 |
| 65% to below 70% | B+ | 3.25 |
| 60% to below 65% | B | 3.00 |
| 55% to below 60% | B- | 2.75 |
| 50% to below 55% | C+ | 2.50 |
| 45% to below 50% | C | 2.25 |
| 40% to below 45% | D | 2.00 |
| Below 40% | F | 0.00 |

### Academic Requirements
*   **Minimum CGPA for Degree:** 2.50.
*   **Attendance Policy:** Minimum 70% attendance required to appear in the final examination. Students with 60%-69% attendance may appear upon payment of a fine. Below 60% is strictly prohibited.
*   **Maximum Duration:** All degree requirements must be completed within 6 academic years.

### Awards & Recognition
*   **Dean’s List:** Awarded to students maintaining an average GPA of 3.75 or above in two consecutive regular terms of an academic year.
*   **University Gold Medal:** Awarded to the 1st position holder in each department with a CGPA ≥ 3.75, provided the course is completed within four consecutive years with no 'F' grades.

### Code of Conduct
*   **Zero Tolerance:** Sexual abuse, harassment, drug abuse, and addiction are strictly prohibited.
*   **Political Activity:** Students are forbidden from forming or joining student political organizations, clubs, or societies other than those authorized by the university.
