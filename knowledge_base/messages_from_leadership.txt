# Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)
# Messages from University Leadership

---

## 1. Message from the Chief of Army Staff
**General Waker-Uz-Zaman, OSP, SGP, psc**  
*Chief of Army Staff, Bangladesh Army*  
*and Chairman, Board of Trustees, BAUST, Saidpur*

Bangladesh Army has been playing a unique role in nation-building and development of the education system in Bangladesh. An enlightened student devotes his/her merit and potentials to bring positive changes and selflessly contributes to the nation. Therefore, we would like to set examples of imparting quality education infused with morality and humanity for all students. In continuation of this effort, Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST), Saidpur was established in 2015.

In an era of rapid technological advancement, the mission of Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST), Saidpur, is to produce graduates who are innovative, adaptable, and ready to meet the global challenges of the 21st century. We are committed to providing a secure, serene, and highly disciplined academic environment that is conducive to focused learning, research, and personal growth. With a distinguished faculty, highly motivating facilities, and a rigorous outcome-based curriculum, BAUST offers an excellent educational experience.

As the Chairman of the Board of Trustees of Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST), Saidpur, I feel honored to shoulder these responsibilities. I would also like to invite students, faculties, and people from all walks of life to be a part of our journey towards transforming a nation enlightened with quality education, morality, and humanity.

*May Allah bless us all.*

## 2. Message from the Vice Chancellor
**Brigadier General Mohammad Mobarak Hossain Majumder, psc**  
*Vice Chancellor*  
*Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST), Saidpur*

Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST), Saidpur was founded in 2015 by the Bangladesh Army with due permission from the Government and UGC. Patronized by the Bangladesh Army, the University aspires to transform itself into a center of excellence in Science, Engineering, Technology, Business Studies, and Arts by offering innovative multi-disciplinary courses and extensive research facilities to the young generation of the country and beyond. The mission of BAUST is to provide state-of-the-art education and research facilities to develop Human Resources (HR) for the socio-economic well-being of society.

To achieve the aforesaid vision and mission, we are oath-bound to provide needs-based education by experienced faculty members so as to produce quality HR who will be well-suited to present-day requirements. Also, ample scopes are created to undertake research and project work which will encourage students to utilize their full potential in innovation and creativity.

Ample opportunity is made available to nurture students' hidden talents and broaden their outlook through various clubs and co-curricular activities. Besides, a number of seminars, symposiums, workshops, and short courses are organized to make students confident and competent and keep pace with the latest technological trends. Above all, our endeavors are dedicated to developing students' sense of responsibility and judgment, preparing them to take responsibilities in various nation-building activities.

*We welcome co-operation in this regard from all concerned.*
