## Department of Mechanical Engineering (ME)
### Laboratory Facilities

The Department of Mechanical Engineering at Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST) is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratories to facilitate practical learning and research. Below is the list of active laboratories:

| Lab Name | Description / Focus |
| :--- | :--- |
| **CAD and Simulation Lab** | Computer-Aided Design and Engineering Simulation |
| **Drawing Lab** | Engineering Graphics and Technical Drawing |
| **Fluid Mechanics and Machinery Lab** | Fluid dynamics, flow measurement, and hydraulic machinery |
| **Heat Engine Lab** | Internal combustion engines and thermal power systems |
| **Machine Shop and Production Process Lab** | Machining, manufacturing, and fabrication processes |
| **Measurement and Instrumentation Lab** | Precision measurement and sensor instrumentation |
| **Mechanics of Machinery Lab** | Kinematics and dynamics of machines |
| **Metallic Materials Lab** | Material testing and metallurgical analysis |
| **Refrigeration and Air Conditioning Lab** | HVAC systems and thermal comfort studies |
| **Strength of Materials Lab** | Stress, strain, and material property testing |
| **Thermodynamics Lab** | Fundamental laws of thermodynamics and heat transfer |
| **Welding Shop** | Industrial joining and welding techniques |

***

**Source:** [BAUST ME Department Laboratories](https://baust.edu.bd/me/facilities/laboratories/)
