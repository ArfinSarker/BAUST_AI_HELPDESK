## Graduate Programs (Masters & MBA Programs at BAUST)
**Overview:** BAUST offers Postgraduate Master's Degree programs through the Department of Business Administration (DBA) and the Department of English. Currently, Engineering departments (CSE, EEE, ME, IPE, CE) only offer 4-year undergraduate B.Sc. Engineering degrees; there is no M.Sc. in engineering offered at BAUST.

### Master of Business Administration (MBA & EMBA)
The Master of Business Administration (MBA) program is offered by the **Department of Business Administration (DBA)** under the **Faculty of Business Studies (FBS)** at BAUST, Saidpur.

BAUST offers two primary MBA tracks:
1. **Regular MBA:**
   - **For BBA Graduates (Business Background):** 1-Year duration (2 Semesters), 36 Credits. Eligibility: BBA degree from any recognized university.
   - **For Other Graduates (Non-Business Background):** 2-Year duration (4 Semesters), 60 Credits. Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline (B.Sc., B.A., B.S.S., etc.).
2. **Executive MBA (EMBA):**
   - Specifically designed for working professionals, corporate executives, and job holders.
   - Duration: 1.5 Years (3 Semesters), 48 Credits.
   - Flexible class schedule held on weekends (Friday/Saturday) and evening slots.
   - Eligibility: Graduation in any discipline with professional work experience.
