# Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)
# Campus Facilities, Infrastructure & Services

---

## 1. Digital Infrastructure & Connectivity
BAUST provides high-speed internet access to support the academic and research needs of students, faculty, and staff.
*   **Availability:** 24/7 access across entire campus and all residential halls.
*   **Coverage:** Entire campus premises, academic buildings, and residential hostels.
*   **Multimedia Facilities:** All classrooms are equipped with modern multimedia projectors to facilitate interactive teaching, visualization of theoretical concepts, animations, and digital learning. Specialized multimedia support is also integrated into programming and simulation laboratories.

## 3. BAUST Daycare Centre
A dedicated facility designed to support the young children of faculty members and staff.
*   **Location:** Ground Floor, Bir Protik Taramon Bibi Hall
*   **Developmental Focus:** Guided instruction, early childhood development, socialization, and creative play.
*   **Curriculum Highlights:** Identification of primary colors, basic food groups, human body parts, alphabets, and numbers.
*   **Facilities:** Dedicated spaces for indoor recreation, rest, and creative craft-making sessions.

## 4. Fire Safety Policy & Infrastructure
BAUST maintains a comprehensive fire safety framework to ensure the highest security for the university community and physical assets.
*   **Risk Management:** Regular fire risk assessments and proactive hazard mitigation strategies.
*   **Staff Training:** Mandatory training on fire safety and the operation of extinguishing equipment.
*   **Emergency Preparedness:** Evacuation plans, emergency exit drills, and periodic safety equipment maintenance.

### Campus Fire Safety Equipment
| Infrastructure Component | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Fire Extinguishers** | Immediate fire suppression throughout all buildings |
| **Fire Hydrants** | High-volume water supply for emergency firefighting |
| **Fire Lanes** | Dedicated unobstructed access routes for emergency vehicles |
| **Fire Alarm Pull Stations** | Immediate acoustic notification of fire emergencies |
| **Emergency Fire Exit Doors** | Safe, illuminated, and rapid evacuation routes |
| **Evacuation Plans** | Strategic guidance posted across all floor levels |

## 5. BAUST Central Library & Resource Guide
The Central Library serves as the premier knowledge hub of BAUST, supporting undergraduate study, faculty teaching, and multidisciplinary research.

*   **Website:** [library.baust.edu.bd](http://library.baust.edu.bd)
*   **Mission:** To provide comprehensive resources and cutting-edge services in support of research, teaching, and learning.
*   **Vision:** To build a world-class Knowledge Resource Centre leveraging 21st-century digital technologies.

### Library Operating Hours
| Season | Period | Timing (Sunday – Thursday) | Saturday Timing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Summer** | 01 March – 14 November | 08:00 AM – 06:00 PM | 10:00 AM – 02:00 PM |
| **Winter** | 15 November – 28 February | 08:30 AM – 04:30 PM | 10:00 AM – 02:00 PM |

### Membership & Access
*   **Eligibility:** All registered students, faculty members, and administrative officers are entitled to library membership.
*   **Registration:** Membership is created automatically upon admission and renewed each semester upon course registration.

### Digital Resources & Consortia
*   **BAS-LiCoB (Library Consortium of Bangladesh):** Access to American Institute of Physics (AIP), APS, ASCE, ASME, Cambridge University Press (CUP), De Gruyter, EBSCO, Oxford Journals, Project MUSE, Springer, and Nature Publishing Group.
*   **UDL Consortium:** Access to 3,500+ WILEY Online Books in perpetual lifetime mode.
*   **Open Access Resources:** AGORA, ARDI, HINARI, DOAB, Bangladesh Journals Online, and 500+ Indian open-access journals.

### Departmental Book Collections
Collections available for all departments: CSE, EEE, ME, IPE, CE, Business Administration (BBA), and English.

### Library Rules & Code of Conduct
*   **Belongings:** Bags, jackets, and umbrellas must be kept in the pigeonholes near the security desk.
*   **Conduct:** Group study, gossiping, eating, drinking, and smoking are strictly prohibited in the reading rooms.
*   **Mobile Policy:** Mobile phones must remain switched off or silent inside the library.
*   **Book Issuance:** No books are issued for home reading within the last 30 minutes of daily closing.

## 6. Campus Engagement Program: "Road to Corporate"
### Collaboration between Epyllion Group & BAUST
The Talent Acquisition Wing of Epyllion Group has launched the "Road to Corporate" program, a strategic campus engagement initiative designed to bridge the gap between academic theory and corporate reality. Through a Memorandum of Understanding (MoU) with the **BAUST Career Counselling and Placement Center (BCCPC)**, this program aims to equip students with professional knowledge, corporate culture exposure, and direct access to internship and Management Trainee opportunities.

### Program Objectives
* **Talent Pipeline:** Create a pool of potential candidates for Epyllion Group’s Management Trainee programs.
* **Professional Development:** Guide students through career-oriented seminars, workshops, and practical knowledge sessions.
* **Corporate Exposure:** Familiarize students with industry standards and professional expectations.

### Collaborative Framework
The BCCPC will facilitate the program by organizing career fairs, counseling sessions, and seminars where Epyllion Group will participate as a key partner. Epyllion Group will provide:
* Internship opportunities.
* Access to Management Trainee job postings.
* Expert-led sessions (online and in-person).
* CV collection for potential recruitment.

### Benefits Overview
| Stakeholder | Key Benefits |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Students** | Professional skill development, internship access, and Management Trainee opportunities. |
| **Epyllion Group** | Access to a high-quality candidate pool and contribution to social/educational sustainability. |
| **BCCPC (BAUST)** | Enhanced industry linkage and ability to place top-performing graduates in reputable roles. |

### Operational Principles
* **Duration:** The MoU is valid for a period of two years.
* **Coordination:** BCCPC will designate a coordinator to liaise with the Epyllion HR team for all events.
* **Engagement:** Epyllion Group may conduct campus-based or online sessions as required.
* **Review:** Both parties will periodically review the progress of the collaboration.
* **Termination:** Either party may terminate the agreement with one month's written notice.

### Dispute Resolution & Confidentiality
* **Dispute Resolution:** Parties commit to resolving differences mutually without litigation.
* **Confidentiality:** Both parties agree to maintain strict confidentiality regarding business affairs, finances, and shared information.
