## Qualifications for Application: 
1. **B.Sc. Engg. in CSE/EEE/ME/CE/ICT:**
   - Applicants who have passed HSC/Alim or equivalent or A level or equivalent examinations with at least GP 3.0 in Mathematics are eligible to apply, if he/she fulfills the following conditions: 
   - HSC/Alim or equivalent examination in Science Group with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry with minimum GPA 3.0 (with additional subject). The total GPA of SSC/Dakhil or equivalent and HSC/Alim or equivalent should be at least 7.0 (with additional subject).  
   - GCE applicants at A-Level with at least two subjects and must have GPA 3.0. The total GPA of GCE O-Level and A-Level should be at least 7.0. However, the candidate must not have any F grade in any subject of both of the levels. 
   - Diploma holders in relevant subjects are eligible to apply with at least GPA 3.0. 
   - Applicants who have passed HSC or equivalent in 2023, 2024 and 2025 are eligible to apply. 
   - **NB:** Applicants who have obtained a total GPA of 9.0 or more together in HSC/Alim or equivalent and SSC/Dakhil or equivalent are eligible for direct admission (without Admission Test).

2. **B.Sc. Engg. in IPE:**
   - **HSC/Alim or Equivalent:** Science Group (Math, Physics, Chemistry) with minimum GPA 3.0 (with additional subject). Combined GPA of SSC/Dakhil and HSC/Alim must be at least 7.0.
   - **GCE (A-Level/O-Level):** Physics, Mathematics, Chemistry, and Functional English required. Minimum GPA 3.0 at A-Level. Combined GPA of O-Level and A-Level must be at least 6.0. No 'F' grade permitted.
   - **Diploma Holders:** Minimum CGPA of 3.00.
   - **Direct Admission:** Applicants with a total GPA of 9.0 or more (combined SSC/Dakhil and HSC/Alim) are eligible for direct admission.

3. **BBA, BBA in AIS and BA (Hons) in English:**
   - Applicants who have passed HSC/Alim or equivalent or A level or equivalent examinations are eligible to apply, if he/she fulfills the following conditions: 
   - HSC/Alim or equivalent examination with minimum GPA of 3.0 (with additional subject). The total GPA of HSC/Alim or equivalent and SSC/Dakhil or equivalent should be at least 6.0 (with additional subject). 
   - GCE O/A Level Examination: At least GPA 3.0 in both GCE O-Level and GCE A-Level. The total GPA in GCE A-Level and O-Level should be at least 6.0. The candidate must not have any F grade in any subject of the both levels. 
   - Applicants who have passed HSC or equivalent in 2023, 2024 and 2025 are eligible to apply. 
   - **NB:** Applicants who have obtained a total GPA of 8.0 or more together in HSC/Alim or equivalent and SSC/Dakhil or equivalent are eligible for direct admission (without Admission Test).

## Marks Distribution for Admission Test: 
- **B.Sc. Engg. in CSE/EEE/ME/IPE/CE/ICT:** Mathematics (40), Physics (30), Chemistry (20), Functional English (10). Total: 100 (1hr, MCQ).
- **BBA & BBA in AIS:** English (35), General Knowledge (30), Analytical Ability (35). Total: 100 (1hr, MCQ).
- **BA (Hons) in English:** English Language and Vocabulary (30), Comprehension and English Writing (30), General Knowledge (Bangladesh Affairs) (20), General Knowledge (Foreign Affairs) (20). Total: 100 (1hr, MCQ).

## Selection & Admission Formalities: 
- **Selection Criteria:** The merit list will be based on: Written test (60%), GPA of SSC/O level/equivalent (20%), and GPA of HSC/A level/equivalent (20%). Selected names will be published on the university website and notice board.
- **Admission Formalities:** Selected candidates must complete admission formalities within the prescribed timeframe; failure to do so results in automatic cancellation.
- **Class Attendance:** Failure to attend classes within two weeks of the academic program's commencement results in admission cancellation and forfeiture of all fees.
- **Waiting List:** Merit-based waiting lists will be published as required.

## Required Documents for Admission:
- Original certificates and transcripts of SSC/Dakhil and HSC/Alim.
- Three copies of recent passport-sized colored photographs (attested by a Class-I Gazetted Officer).
- Character certificate from the head of the last attended institute.
- Nationality certificate from UP Chairman/Municipality/City Corporation.
- **Quota-Specific:** Freedom Fighter certificate (Ministry of Freedom Fighters Affairs), Tribal Citizen certificate (UP Chairman/DC), or Military Personnel certificate (Commanding Officer/CORO/Naval/Air Secretary).

## Medical & Dope Test: 
Candidates must undergo a medical check-up at the BAUST Medical Center. Required test reports: Blood Group, CBC, HBs.Ag, and Urine R/E. BAUST ensures a drug-free campus; students must undergo a Dope Test during admission.

# Admission Cancellation Policy and Refund Schedule

## Admission Cancellation Guidelines
- **General Policy:** The cancellation of admission is normally not permissible. However, under special circumstances, cancellation may be considered based on established guidelines.
- **Key Conditions:**
  - Applies to special circumstances only.
  - Refund amount depends on the timing of the cancellation.
  - Caution money is treated separately.
- **Reference:** SOP, Academic Guideline, October 2020.
- **Contact:** For any cancellation request, please contact the Admission Office.

## Refund Schedule
Refundable amounts categorized by the timing of the cancellation:

| Ser | Description | Refundable Amount |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Before commencement of the classes | 100% of the Establishment Fees and 100% of the Semester Fee |
| 2 | Within the 1st week of commencement of the classes | The Caution Money, 75% of the Establishment Fee, and the Semester Fee |
| 3 | Within 2 weeks of commencement of the classes | The Caution Money, 50% of the Establishment Fee, and the Semester Fee |
| 4 | After 2 weeks of commencement of the classes | Caution Money only |
