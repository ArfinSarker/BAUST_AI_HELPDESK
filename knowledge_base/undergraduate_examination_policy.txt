# Undergraduate Examination Policy & Academic Regulations
# Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)

## Academic Calendar & Term Structure
*   Each academic year consists of two regular terms (Winter and Summer).
*   Each term spans **26 weeks**:
    *   7 weeks: Classes (Part 1)
    *   1 week: Mid-term Examination
    *   7 weeks: Classes (Part 2)
    *   2 weeks: Preparatory Leave (PL)
    *   3 weeks: Final Term Examination
    *   3 weeks: Industrial Training / Term Vacation
    *   2 weeks: Referred / Backlog Examination & Result Publication

## Grading Scheme & Grade Point Average (GPA)

| Numerical Marks | Letter Grade | Grade Point | Assessment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **80% and above** | **A+** | **4.00** | Outstanding |
| **75% to 79%** | **A** | **3.75** | Excellent |
| **70% to 74%** | **A-** | **3.50** | Very Good |
| **65% to 69%** | **B+** | **3.25** | Good |
| **60% to 64%** | **B** | **3.00** | Satisfactory |
| **55% to 59%** | **B-** | **2.75** | Above Average |
| **50% to 54%** | **C+** | **2.50** | Average |
| **45% to 49%** | **C** | **2.25** | Below Average |
| **40% to 44%** | **D** | **2.00** | Pass |
| **Less than 40%** | **F** | **0.00** | Fail |

---

## Term Promotion & Academic Progression Rules
1. **Theory Courses:** A student must pass a minimum required number of theory courses each term. A maximum of **2 failed theory courses** is allowed to be carried forward as Referred/Backlog for promotion to the next term.
2. **Sessional (Laboratory) Courses:** Students **MUST pass all sessional/laboratory courses**. Failing any sessional course results in year loss or term retention.
3. **Degree Completion Criteria:**
   *   Minimum Cumulative Grade Point Average (CGPA) required for graduation: **2.20**.
   *   Maximum time limit to complete the 4-year B.Sc. Engineering degree: **6 academic years**.
