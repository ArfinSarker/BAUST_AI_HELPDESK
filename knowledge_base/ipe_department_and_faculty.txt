# Department of Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE)
# Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)

## Department Overview
The Department of Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE) at BAUST provides an advanced, industry-aligned curriculum combining core mechanical/manufacturing engineering principles with modern industrial management, automation, operations research, and quality engineering.
