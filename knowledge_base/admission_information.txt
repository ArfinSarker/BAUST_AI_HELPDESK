## Qualifications for Application: 
1. **B.Sc. Engg. in CSE/EEE/ME/IPE/CE/ICT:**
   - Applicants who have passed HSC/Alim or equivalent or A level or equivalent examinations with at least GP 3.0 in Mathematics are eligible to apply, if he/she fulfills the following conditions: 
   - HSC/Alim or equivalent examination in Science Group with Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry with minimum GPA 3.0 (with additional subject). The total GPA of SSC/Dakhil or equivalent and HSC/Alim or equivalent should be at least 7.0 (with additional subject).  
   - GCE applicants at A-Level with at least two subjects and must have GPA 3.0. The total GPA of GCE O-Level and A-Level should be at least 7.0. However, the candidate must not have any F grade in any subject of both of the levels. 
   - Diploma holders in relevant subjects are eligible to apply with at least GPA 3.0. 
   - Applicants who have passed HSC or equivalent in 2023, 2024 and 2025 are eligible to apply. 
   - **NB:** Applicants who have obtained a total GPA of 9.0 or more together in HSC/Alim or equivalent and SSC/Dakhil or equivalent are eligible for direct admission (without Admission Test).

2. **BBA, BBA in AIS and BA (Hons) in English:**
   - Applicants who have passed HSC/Alim or equivalent or A level or equivalent examinations are eligible to apply, if he/she fulfills the following conditions: 
   - HSC/Alim or equivalent examination with minimum GPA of 3.0 (with additional subject). The total GPA of HSC/Alim or equivalent and SSC/Dakhil or equivalent should be at least 6.0 (with additional subject). 
   - GCE O/A Level Examination: At least GPA 3.0 in both GCE O-Level and GCE A-Level. The total GPA in GCE A-Level and O-Level should be at least 6.0. The candidate must not have any F grade in any subject of the both levels. 
   - Applicants who have passed HSC or equivalent in 2023, 2024 and 2025 are eligible to apply. 
   - **NB:** Applicants who have obtained a total GPA of 8.0 or more together in HSC/Alim or equivalent and SSC/Dakhil or equivalent are eligible for direct admission (without Admission Test).

## Marks Distribution for Admission Test: 
- **B.Sc. Engg. in CSE/EEE/ME/IPE/CE/ICT:** Mathematics (40), Physics (30), Chemistry (20), Functional English (10). Total: 100 (1hr, MCQ).
- **BBA & BBA in AIS:** English (35), General Knowledge (30), Analytical Ability (35). Total: 100 (1hr, MCQ).
- **BA (Hons) in English:** English Language and Vocabulary (30), Comprehension and English Writing (30), General Knowledge (Bangladesh Affairs) (20), General Knowledge (Foreign Affairs) (20). Total: 100 (1hr, MCQ).

## Selection Criteria: 
The merit list will be based on: Written test (60%), GPA of SSC/O level/equivalent (20%), and GPA of HSC/A level/equivalent (20%). Selected names will be published on the university website and notice board.

## Medical & Dope Test: 
Candidates must deposit Blood Group, CBC, HBs.Ag, and Urine R/E test reports during admission. BAUST ensures a drug-free campus; students must undergo a Dope Test during admission.
