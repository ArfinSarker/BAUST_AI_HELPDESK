## General Instructions and Policies

1. **Moon Sighting:** Dates marked with an asterisk (*) are subject to the sighting of the moon.
2. **Program Details:** Details of all programmatic activities will be published separately.
3. **Make-up Classes:** Make-up classes for any holidays will be arranged by the respective course teachers.
4. **Co-Curricular Timeline:** All club activities, seminars, and tournaments must be completed by the 12th week of the semester.
5. **Freshers' Reception:** Freshers' reception should be organized during the 2nd week of the semester.
