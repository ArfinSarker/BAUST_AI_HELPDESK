# Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)

## Brief History & Establishment
BAUST is a private university sponsored by the Bangladesh Army Welfare Trust. Bangladesh Army established BAUST on 15 February 2015 at Saidpur Cantonment, and it was inaugurated by the Hon’ble Minister of Cultural Affairs of Bangladesh on the same date.

The intention to establish BAUST is to spread quality technical and scientific education to the northern and remote regions of the country.
BAUST received formal approval to commence its academic activities from the Ministry of Education and the University Grants Commission (UGC) of Bangladesh on 17 February 2015. It launched its first academic session with four departments (CSE, EEE, ME, and Arts & Sciences) under 03 faculties. At present, BAUST functions with 10 departments under 05 faculties.

BAUST is in full compliance with the Private University Act 2010 and statutory guidelines issued by the Army Welfare Trust, Ministry of Education, and UGC.

---

## Why BAUST?
BAUST stands as a premier higher education institution committed to academic rigor, state-of-the-art laboratory training, and holistic character development. Our core philosophy is anchored on three foundational pillars: **Discipline, Knowledge, and Morality.**

By bridging the gap between rigorous technical education and moral leadership, BAUST ensures that graduates are not only technically proficient engineers and managers but also socially responsible citizens dedicated to nation-building and global innovation.

---

## Vision
The University aspires to transform into a center of excellence in Science, Engineering, Technology, Business Studies, and Humanities by providing innovative, multi-disciplinary undergraduate and graduate programs to the young generation of Bangladesh and beyond.

---

## Mission
*   **Educational Excellence:** Providing state-of-the-art education to achieve deep disciplinary mastery, problem-solving prowess, leadership acumen, and modern professional skills.
*   **Collaborative Environment:** Fostering a collaborative ecosystem enabling the free exchange of ideas, advanced research, innovation, and industry partnerships.
*   **Sustainability & Service:** Developing sustainable technologies and socially responsive graduates to serve the nation and humanity.

---

## Objectives
*   **Need-Based Programs:** Offer cutting-edge academic programs across Science, Engineering, Business, and Humanities.
*   **Excellence in Teaching:** Maintain modern outcome-based education (OBE) instructional methodologies.
*   **Human Resource Development:** Produce global-standard engineers, researchers, and entrepreneurs with strong critical thinking.
*   **Global Collaboration:** Establish partnerships, MoUs, and exchange programs with leading national and international universities.
*   **Core Values:** Instill integrity, openness, self-discipline, respect, and social responsibility across the student body.
