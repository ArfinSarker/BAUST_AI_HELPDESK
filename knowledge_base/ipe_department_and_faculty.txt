# Department of Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE)
# Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)

**Document Version:** April 2026 (Updated based on 18th Academic Council Meeting)

## Department Overview
The Department of Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE) at BAUST, located in Saidpur Cantonment, Saidpur, Nilphamari, is a rapidly developing academic unit dedicated to quality education, research, and industrial collaboration. It provides an advanced, industry-aligned curriculum combining core mechanical/manufacturing engineering principles with modern industrial management, automation, operations research, and quality engineering. The department actively bridges the gap between academia and industry by providing specialized advisory, consultancy, research, and testing services to government and private organizations.

## General Information
### Introduction
BAUST was established on 15 February 2015 by the Bangladesh Armed Forces to provide high-quality technical education. The university offers four-year Bachelor of Science (B.Sc.) degrees in various engineering disciplines including CSE, EEE, ME, IPE, and CE.

### Admission Qualifications (B.Sc. Engg.)
1.  **HSC/Alim:** Minimum GPA 3.0 (with additional subject).
2.  **SSC/Dakhil:** Minimum GPA 3.0.
3.  **Combined GPA:** Minimum 7.0 (SSC + HSC).
4.  **GCE O/A Level:** Minimum C Grade in 5 O-Level subjects; Minimum C Grade in 2 A-Level subjects (Math, Physics/Chemistry). Total GPA 6.0.
5.  **Diploma Holders:** Minimum CGPA 3.0 in Diploma and GPA 3.0 in SSC.
6.  **Direct Admission:** Applicants with a total GPA of 9.0 or more in SSC/HSC are eligible for direct admission.
