# Undergraduate Examination Policy & Academic Regulations
# Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST)
**Saidpur Cantonment, Saidpur, Nilphamari**

---

## Overview
BAUST is a private university sponsored by the Bangladesh Army Welfare Trust (est. 15 Feb 2015). This policy governs the academic conduct, evaluation, and examination procedures for all undergraduate programs under the Private University Act 2010.

---

## Academic Calendar & Term Structure
Each academic level consists of two regular terms (Term-I and Term-II). Each term spans **26 weeks**:

| Sl. No. | Events | Duration (Weeks) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 1 | Classes before Mid Term | 7 |
| 2 | Mid Term Examination | 1 |
| 3 | Mid Term Break/ Vacation | 1 |
| 4 | Classes after Mid Term | 7 |
| 5 | Preparatory Leave for Term Final | 2 |
| 6 | Term Final Examination | 3 |
| 7 | Industrial Attachment/Vacation/Result Pub. | 3 |
| 8 | Referred/ Improvement/ Backlog Exam | 2 |

---

## Course Pattern & Credit Structure
*   **Designation System:** Two to four-letter code + four-digit number.
    *   1st digit: Level | 2nd digit: Term | 3rd digit: Dept use | 4th digit: Area/Group.
    *   Last digit: Odd (Theory), Even (Sessional).
*   **Credit Assignment:**
    *   Theoretical: 1 lecture hour/week = 1 credit hr.
    *   Sessional/Laboratory: 2 class hours/week = 1 credit hr.

## Distribution of Marks
### Theory Courses
*   Class Performance: 5% | Class Tests: 15% | Assignments/Presentations: 5% | Mid-Term: 15% | Final Exam: 60%

### Sessional Courses
*   **Lab-based:** Attendance 25%, Report 15%, Mid-term 20%, Final 30%, Viva 10%.
*   **Project-based:** Attendance 25%, Report/Programming 15%, Mid-term 20%, Final 30%, Viva/Presentation 10%.

---

## Examination & Attendance Policies
*   **Attendance Requirements:**
    *   **75% attendance:** Mandatory to sit for the final exam.
    *   **60% - 74% attendance:** Fine required to sit for the final exam.
    *   **Below 60%:** Not allowed to sit for the final exam.
*   **Examination Coordination:** The Office of the Controller of Examination is the central coordinating body for all examinations at BAUST.
*   **Conduct:** No name on answer scripts, no electronic devices. Unfair means result in expulsion/cancellation.
*   **Rounding:** Marks rounded to nearest whole number; GPA/CGPA rounded to two decimal places.

---

## Term Promotion & Academic Progression Rules
1. **Theory Courses:** Must pass 2/4, 3/5, or 4/6 courses.
2. **Sessional (Laboratory) Courses:** Maximum two failed courses allowed.
3. **Degree Completion Criteria:**
   *   Minimum CGPA required for graduation: **2.20**.
   *   Maximum time limit: **6 academic years (12 terms)**.
   *   **Honors:** CGPA 3.75+ (Engineering/BBA).
   *   **Distinction:** CGPA 3.75+ (English).
   *   **Dean's List:** GPA 3.75+ in two consecutive terms.
   *   **Gold Medal:** 1st position in batch, CGPA ≥ 3.75, no 'F' grades, and no disciplinary records.

---

## Examination Fees & Registration
*   **Mid-Term Make-up:** Tk 500.
*   **Referred/Improvement/Backlog:** Registration Fee: Tk 1,000/course; New continuous assessment for backlog: Tk 5,000/course.
*   **Special Backlog:** Available for final-year students with max 12 credits remaining (30 days after final results).
*   **Late Registration:** Penalty of Tk 500 per week.

## Admission Information
### 3.1 Eligibility Criteria (B.Sc. Engg.)
*   **SSC/HSC:** Minimum GPA 3.5 in both, with a total minimum GPA of 7.5.
*   **O/A Level:** Minimum 5 subjects in O Level and 3 subjects (Math, Physics, Chemistry) in A Level with minimum C grade in all subjects.
*   **Break of Study:** Candidates with more than one year break are ineligible.

### 3.2 Admission Test Structure
| Subject | Weightage |
| :--- | :--- |
| Mathematics | 30% |
| Physics | 30% |
| Chemistry | 30% |
| English | 10% |

### 3.3 Final Selection Criteria
*   **Written Admission Test:** 75% | **GPA of SSC/Equivalent:** 10% | **GPA of HSC/Equivalent:** 15%
