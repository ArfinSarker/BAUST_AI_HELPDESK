# Department of Civil Engineering (CE) - BAUST

## Overview
Civil engineering is the backbone of modern civilization—one of the oldest and most far-reaching disciplines in engineering. Civil engineers are the architects of infrastructure, shaping the physical world while ensuring environmental sustainability. From towering skyscrapers to sprawling dams, from cutting-edge suspension bridges to offshore drilling platforms, and from flood management to environmental protection, civil engineers make it all possible. They tackle challenges like earthquake resistance, pollution control, and sustainable development, all while managing projects and ensuring quality.

## Civil Engineering at BAUST
The Department of Civil Engineering (CE) at BAUST embarked on its mission in December 2019. The department offers a comprehensive fouryear B.Sc. degree in Civil Engineering, utilizing an **Outcome-Based Education (OBE)** system.

## Laboratory Facilities
The Department of Civil Engineering provides specialized laboratory facilities to bridge theoretical knowledge with practical application:

1.  **Practical Surveying Lab:** Focuses on chain, compass, and plane table surveying, leveling, contouring, theodolite, tacheometry, and curve/house setting.
2.  **Civil Engineering Drawing Lab:** Covers manual drafting, geometric figures, projections, and building design (plan, elevation, and section) using traditional and CAD methods.
3.  **Engineering Materials Lab (Concrete Lab):** Conducts tests on cement, aggregates, concrete, and bricks, including compressive strength, sieve analysis, and durability tests.
4.  **Structural Mechanics Lab (SM Lab):** Analyzes structural behavior under load, including tension, compression, shear, hardness, and buckling tests.
5.  **Open Channel Flow Lab:** Studies fluid behavior in rivers and canals, focusing on energy losses, flow visualization, and hydraulic structure design.
6.  **Fluid Mechanics Lab:** Validates Bernoulli’s theorem and tests flow through orifices, weirs, and pipes.
7.  **Computer Lab:** Utilizes software such as MATLAB, AutoCAD, ETABS, and SAP 2000 for engineering analysis, design, and programming.
8.  **Mechanical Workshop Lab:** Provides hands-on training in fabrication, welding, cutting, and the use of power tools and measuring instruments.

2.  **General Electives:** 3 additional Theory courses from Options I to VI (max 1 per group) + 1 additional Sessional from corresponding groups.

### Important Academic Notes
*   **Thesis/Capstone:** CE 4000 and CE 4002 are conducted throughout the 4th year (4-I and 4-II).
*   **Industrial Attachment:** CE 4170 commences after the 3-II final examination for a duration of 2-3 weeks.

## Department of Civil Engineering Alumni Association (BAUST)

### Mission Statement
To establish a dynamic and forward-looking alumni network for the Department of Civil Engineering at BAUST, Saidpur—one that fosters social, cultural, academic, and professional engagement among graduates. Our mission is to strengthen the bonds of unity and fellowship among alumni, while also supporting the continuous development of our department into a leading center of excellence in civil and infrastructure engineering. Through collaboration and contribution, we aim to play an active role in national development and in achieving the department’s vision. Our commitment is to remain connected with our alma mater and create meaningful opportunities for both current students and alumni.

### Core Activities
*   **Academic & Professional:** Organizing seminars, technical workshops, training sessions, and supporting academic research/innovation projects.
*   **Career Development:** Hosting job fairs, recruitment events, career counseling, and mentoring programs.
*   **Networking & Social:** Coordinating educational field trips, industrial visits, reunions, departmental picnics, and annual dinners.
*   **Community & Engagement:** Conducting quiz contests, technical competitions, project showcases, cultural activities, and community service programs (e.g., blood donation drives).
