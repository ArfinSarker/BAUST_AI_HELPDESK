# Department of Industrial and Production Engineering (IPE) - Laboratory Facilities

The Department of IPE at BAUST is equipped with state-of-the-art laboratory facilities designed to bridge the gap between theoretical knowledge and real-world industrial applications. Students gain hands-on experience in manufacturing processes, automation (PLC/Robotics), and advanced simulation software (CAD/CAM, MATLAB, ANSYS, Arena).

## Detailed Laboratory Descriptions

### Materials Handling & Maintenance Engineering Lab
Equipped with modern material hauling appliances including screw, belt, roller, and chain conveyors. Designed to teach students the essence of industrial environments and the design of custom material handling systems and facility layouts.

### Measurement, Instrumentation and Quality Control Lab
A hub for practical innovation featuring Robotics Trainers (ROBOTEK II), Programmable Logic Controllers (PLC), Large Tool Maker’s Microscope, Temperature and Pressure Measurement Instruments, Industrial Mechanism Trainers, and Propeller Assembly.

### Foundry and Welding Shop
Located at the Newly Extended Campus, this facility supports metal casting, molding, melting, and various welding processes (arc and gas). It focuses on industrial safety procedures and material behavior.

### Metallurgy Lab
Contains ten modern metallurgical microscopes for internal grain structure analysis and a modern furnace for heat treatment and property testing at varying temperatures.

### Engineering Drawing Lab
Provides 60 well-furnished drawing tables. Students utilize manual drafting tools including T-squares, French curves, compasses, and precision scales to define engineering requirements.

### Strength of Materials Lab
Equipped with a Universal Testing Machine (UTM), Charpy Impact Testing Machine, and other instruments to conduct tensile, compression, bending, impact, and hardness tests.

### Fluid Mechanics and Machinery Lab
Focuses on fluid statics and dynamics, flow measurement, pipe flow losses, and the performance characteristics of pumps and turbines.

### Thermodynamics & Heat Transfer Lab
Features experimental setups for boilers, engines, refrigeration and air-conditioning systems, heat exchangers, and studies on conduction, convection, and radiation.

### Mechanics of Machinery Lab
Utilizes demonstration models to analyze cams and followers, gear trains, governors, flywheels, and the balancing of rotating/reciprocating masses.

### Ergonomics & Safety Management Lab
Equipped with instruments to measure workplace vibration, noise, illumination, and temperature. Focuses on human–machine interaction and occupational health and safety (OHS) risk assessment.

### Machine Shop and Production Process Lab
Facilitates the production of finished parts using lathes, shapers, planners, and milling machines, allowing students to operate industrial-grade manufacturing tools.

### CAD and Simulation Lab
Dedicated to computer-aided design and manufacturing.
*   **Software Suite:** ANSYS 14.0, AutoCAD 2017, SOLIDWORKS 2016, MATLAB 2014.
*   **Hardware:** LCD Mounting Projector.
