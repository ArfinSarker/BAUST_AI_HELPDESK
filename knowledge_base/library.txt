# BAUST Central Library: Information & Resource Guide

## Overview
The Bangladesh Army University of Science and Technology (BAUST) Library serves as the institution's knowledge hub, supporting research, teaching, and learning. It houses a vast collection of books, journals, monographs, and periodicals.

*   **Website:** [library.baust.edu.bd](http://library.baust.edu.bd)
*   **Mission:** To provide comprehensive resources and services in support of the research, teaching, and learning needs of the University community.
*   **Vision:** To build a world-class Knowledge Resource Centre using 21st-century technological developments.

## Membership & Access
*   **Eligibility:** All students, faculty members, and officers are entitled to library membership.
*   **Registration:** Membership is created automatically upon admission. Validity is contingent upon course registration for each semester.

## Library Rules & Regulations
*   **Personal Belongings:** Bags, coats, jackets, and umbrellas must be kept in the pigeonholes near the security desk.
*   **Conduct:** Group study, gossiping, discussions, eating, drinking, and smoking are strictly prohibited.
*   **Equipment Care:** Users must handle furniture, books, journals, and computers with care and leave the area tidy.
*   **Mobile Policy:** Mobile phones are not permitted inside the library.
*   **Issuance:** No books shall be issued for reading room or home use within the last 30 minutes of daily closing.
